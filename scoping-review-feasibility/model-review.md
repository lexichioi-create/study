Muharikah, A., Li, M., & Roberts, J. (2022). Scoping review of teaching and learning of English as an additional language among autistic individuals. *Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction, 19*(2), 1–35. https://doi.org/10.32890/mjli2022.19.2.1

## 고른 이유

- **방법이 같다**: JBI 매뉴얼과 PRISMA-ScR을 따른 스코핑 리뷰다.
- **주제가 가깝다**: 모국어가 영어가 아닌 자폐 학습자의 영어 학습을 다룬다. 포함 연구의 65%가 아시아 연구다.
- **절차가 명시적이다**: 검색식 원문, 6개 DB와 Google Scholar, 2단계 2인 선별, Excel 기반 데이터 차팅과 2차 검토, PRISMA-ScR 흐름도(2,346건 → 18편)가 모두 나와 있다.
- **공백 근거가 된다**: 교실 중심, 자폐만, 2020년 7월까지의 연구만 다뤘다.

> We adapted the protocol used in Muharikah et al. (2022) by modifying the eligibility criteria, search strategy, and data-charting items to align with the current review question.

**과제 제출용 문단**

> Model review: Muharikah, A., Li, M., & Roberts, J. (2022). Scoping review of teaching and learning of English as an additional language among autistic individuals. *Malaysian Journal of Learning and Instruction, 19*(2), 1–35. https://doi.org/10.32890/mjli2022.19.2.1
>
> I chose this review because it uses the approach I plan to use (a scoping review following the JBI manual and PRISMA-ScR) and addresses a closely related topic: English learning by autistic learners whose first language is not English. Its explicit search strings, two-stage screening by two reviewers, and data-charting table can be adapted directly to my protocol. Because it covered classroom learning among autistic individuals only (studies published before July 2020), it also shows the gap my review will address: English learning beyond the classroom among learners with autism, ADHD, or dyslexia.
